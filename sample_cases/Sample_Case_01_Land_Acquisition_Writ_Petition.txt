AFFIDAVIT IN REPLY ON BEHALF OF RESPONDENT NO. 1 (MMRDA)

I, Rajesh V. Sharma, aged 52 years, Indian Inhabitant, working as Deputy Metropolitan Commissioner at Mumbai Metropolitan Region Development Authority (MMRDA), having office at BKC, Mumbai - 400051, do hereby solemnly affirm and state as under:

1. I am working as Deputy Metropolitan Commissioner with Respondent No. 1 and I am fully conversant with the facts and circumstances of the present case. I am competent to depose to this Affidavit on behalf of Respondent No. 1.

2. At the outset, I deny each and every allegation, contention, and statement made in the Writ Petition under reply, save and except what is specifically admitted herein.

3. PRELIMINARY OBJECTIONS:
   (a) The Writ Petition is not maintainable as the Petitioner has suppressed material facts regarding the statutory notice dated 14th November 2025 issued under Section 125 of the MRTP Act, 1966.
   (b) The Petitioner has an alternate and efficacious remedy by way of statutory appeal under Section 127 of the Maharashtra Regional and Town Planning Act, 1966.

4. PARAGRAPH-WISE REPLY:
   (a) With reference to Paragraph 1 to 4 of the Petition, I state that the land bearing Survey No. 142 at Kanjurmarg was designated for Public Utility Infrastructure under the Approved Development Plan 2034 notified on 8th January 2018.
   (b) With reference to Paragraph 5 of the Petition, it is denied that the possession was taken unlawfully on 10th February 2026. The possession was handed over voluntarily by the land owners under Possession Receipt marked as Exhibit R-1.

5. PRAYER:
   In view of the facts stated hereinabove, Respondent No. 1 respectfully prays that:
   (a) The Writ Petition be dismissed with exemplary costs;
   (b) Pass such further order or orders as this Hon'ble Court may deem fit and proper in the interest of justice.
